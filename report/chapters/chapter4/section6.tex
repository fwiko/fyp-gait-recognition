\section{Implementation Summary}
\label{sec:implementation-summary}

This chapter has presented the design and implementation of a \ac{poc} \ac{gr} authentication based access control system.
The system was designed to be a modular, scalable, and secure solution that could be easily deployed as a \ac{pacs}.

The solution consists of two components: the \ac{acc} and the \ac{grs}, with the \ac{acc} responsible for movement data collection and abstraction into \acp{gei}, and the \ac{grs} responsible for performing classification, and managing identities and their associated access configuration, with their final structure from a code perspective presented in Appendix \ref{app:client-code-architecture} and Appendix \ref{app:server-code-architecture} respectively.

The following section will cover functional, performance, and user testing of the system, based on the plans defined in Section \ref{sec:implementation-and-test-planning}.
This is followed by an assessment of how objectives were met, and an evaluation of the feasibility of implementation in a real-world setting.
