\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:results-and-evaluation-introduction}

This chapter presents the results and evaluation of the overall gait-based access control system \ac{poc} developed as a result of this project.

The following sections use the test plans defined in Chapter \ref{chap:new-ideas} to evaluate both the \ac{acc} and \ac{grs} components of the system against functional and non-functional requirements.
Performance and accuracy testing is also performed to assess the system's capabilities, with results compared to the accuracies of existing biometric identification technology.

User feedback, collected from six (6) volunteers during testing and demonstrations of the \ac{poc} is also presented, with comparisons made to the public opinion survey conducted in Chapter \ref{chap:context}.

Finally, the objectives of the project are revisited to determine if they have been met, concluding with an assessment of the feasibility of implementing a similar system in a real-world setting.
