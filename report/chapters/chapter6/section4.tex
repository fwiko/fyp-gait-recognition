\section{Personal Reflection and Aspirations}

I am proud of the work I have produced over the course of this project, 
I feel that I have grown significantly in an academic, professional, and personal capacity.

Gaining experience in a once foreign field of research (both \ac{ml} for which I have partaken in no previous modules, and \ac{gr} for which I had no previous exposure to) has been a great experience, 
and I feel that I have a much deeper understanding of the subject and many attributing concepts than when I started.

My interest in computer security, high-performance computing/data science, software development, and general problem-solving has been further solidified,
and I plan to pursue a career that encompasses these areas where possible.

Completing a project that produced technology with a potential ethical and societal impact has been interesting.
I have had to consider the potential implications caused by misuse of the technology I have developed from both a philosophical and legal perspective, which is a change of scenery from the technical aspects of my studies.

This final year project has allowed me to apply knowledge gained throughout my studies and placement year, 
and has necessitated the exploration of unfamiliar areas --- most notably \ac{ml}, and a significant amount of mathematics.
I appreciate the opportunity provided by the university and support received from my supervisor, family, and friends.
I look forward to applying what I have learned not only from this project, but from the studies I have completed in parallel in my future career.
